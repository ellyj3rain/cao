# B12 Cultural Cognition Causal Contract

Date: 2026-08-13 UTC / 2026-08-12 to 2026-08-13 PDT

Culture owns population distributions over questions. Ideoligion owns doctrine. Political Beliefs own what people consider proper about rule and collective arrangements. Current order and organizations own what is instituted. Knowledge records own propositions and access. Actions and practices remain factual conduct.

## Common path

`Culture question row → CACultureQuestionDistribution → deterministic pawn attitude → represented consumer → persisted consumer state/history`

Each distribution persists a mean, nonzero spread, salience, norm strength, public visibility, source confidence, divergence tolerance, optional subgroup mixture, provenance, source identity, and evidence signature. Private position is materialized deterministically from world, Culture, subgroup, pawn, question, and epoch identities. The bounded distribution is calibrated so its realized population mean remains the authored center when spread changes. Doctrine contributes injunctive pressure after materialization; it never rewrites authored Culture.

## Independent neighboring controls

Changing a Culture question does not create a relationship, office, institution, faction, settlement, practice, stockpile, research result, political mechanism, or Ideoligion precept. Within-group spread changes population variance without changing the authored center. Subgroup separation changes represented subgroup offsets without manufacturing a dissent category. Salience changes moral conviction; norm strength and divergence tolerance change expected enforcement; source confidence changes knowledge confidence and uncertainty; visibility changes inspectable public expression. Political and institutional state develop from represented facts at their own owners and cadences. Culture changes appraisal and the selection of discretionary CA-originated action; it never revokes native legality or direct, relayed, or save-restored operator intent.

An influence edge records contact separately from what was observed. A Culture
question or political axis affects perceived neighbors only while that exact
subject has a current exposure record. Proximity, relation, and faction
membership do not reveal every position. Learned political issue links require
at least sixteen represented pawns, a strong cross-pawn correlation, finite-
sample shrinkage, family-wise null control across the complete cross-axis option
family, and current evidence; a vanished association removes its link.

Event actors and epistemic sources remain separate. Direct witnesses source
their own observation. A represented reporting body records each distinct
reporter-to-holder route only when reporter and holder are different pawns,
retains whether each route was direct observation or a report, delivers each
route to proposition knowledge once,
and lets independent reporters accumulate bounded corroboration without
repeating the holder's social or political reaction.
