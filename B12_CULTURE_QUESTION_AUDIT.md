# B12 Culture Question Audit

Date: 2026-08-13 UTC / 2026-08-12 to 2026-08-13 PDT

This audit classifies every social subject in the B11 production registry under the B12 model. A social subject remains a factual referent for acts, practices, institutions, relationships, or material state. It becomes a Culture question only when the old approval dimension has an exact, ordered construct in the current thirteen-question registry. The migration preserves every old record as evidence, including normality, prestige, salience, weight, source, and signature. It does not translate an unmatched subject into a convenient substitute.

## Migration rule

For an exact adapter, B12 maps weighted B11 approval to the distribution mean and weighted salience to salience. The other former dimensions remain in `CACultureLegacyEvidence`; they do not acquire new semantics. For an unmatched subject, the complete source record remains migration evidence and no `CACultureQuestionDistribution` is created. Current Culture schema 10 never writes `inheritedMeanings` or `localMeanings`.

The current authored fixture demonstrates both paths: 26 former meaning records become 22 exact question distributions across eight Culture records, while the unmatched compulsory-transfer record remains explicit evidence.
