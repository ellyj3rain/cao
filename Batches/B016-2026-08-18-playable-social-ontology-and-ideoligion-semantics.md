## Record

B16 establishes one explicit ownership split across playable social facts.
Native Ideoligion owns doctrine, sacred and prohibited conduct, rituals, memes,
precepts, roles, and prescriptions. Culture owns population appraisal,
distribution, disagreement, confidence, and historical drift. Political Order
owns beliefs about legitimate political and economic arrangements. Represented
institutions own mechanisms actually in force. Practice owns repeated conduct.
These facts can agree or conflict without one overwriting another, and RimWorld
remains the executor of its native mechanics.

The Culture registry expands from B13's historical twenty-four-question boundary
to forty-eight questions in twelve player decision categories. The expansion is
the result of auditing the playable ontology from Core and DLC definitions,
current CA mechanics, and explicitly supported mod mechanics back toward their
semantic owners. It is not a quota and does not create one Culture question per
precept. Several native definitions can evidence one cultural construct, while
definitions that are doctrinal or ritual without a separate culturally variable
meaning remain exclusively Ideoligion-owned.

`CAIdeoligionSemanticAdapterRegistry` admits only exact package ID, definition
kind, and `defName` mappings. Known Core, Ideology, Biotech, Odyssey, and audited
`Ideology: More Precepts` definitions can apply bounded evidence pressure to a
Culture question. Unknown mod definitions remain native facts; labels,
descriptions, and similar names cannot manufacture semantics. This preserves
native Ideoligion while exposing additional mechanically consequential social
vocabulary through one explicit extension seam.

`CANativeCultureEventAdapterRegistry` observes exact `HistoryEventDef` events in
a postfix after native execution. The map Culture-longitudinal owner retains a
bounded ledger with exact package, event, practice, pawn, faction, map, and tick
provenance. One occurrence remains an occurrence. Only repeated represented
conduct can become practice evidence, and neither occurrence nor practice
proves Culture approval. Cannibalism can therefore be separately prescribed by
Ideoligion, appraised by Culture, and observed as actual eating or butchery.

Campaign catalog 5 advances Culture question registry 3, map Culture-
longitudinal schema 3, and faction, founding, and regional owner schema 4.
Migration preserves every compatible prior Culture row and adds only missing
B16 questions at neutral, low-confidence, explicitly unobserved state in each
represented population scope. Affected owners validate before catalog
publication. The active/mirror authored fixture is converted atomically without
changing its composition. Its one historical sparse row whose population is no
longer a constituent of that settlement is removed from live coverage and
retained with its exact identity and signature as legacy evidence. Retained
B10-B15 suites verify that the expanded ontology does not regress the existing
creation, Society, technology, or regional contracts.
