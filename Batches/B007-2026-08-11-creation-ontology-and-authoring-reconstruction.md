## Record

B7 reconstructs the creation ontology at its ownership boundary. Simulation
complexity no longer becomes equivalent authoring complexity. Starting Region
retains the selected region, arrival area, map, factions, settlements, locations,
population composition and sources, ownership, relations, provisions, and sparse
concrete starting exceptions. Settlement scale, transport access, services, civic
capacity, facilities, infrastructure, research capability, and other material
outcomes are derived from current population, land, geography, technology,
knowledge, institutions, economy, trade, material state, and history. The global
Compact, Standard, and Expanded authoring policy and repeated local detail toggles
are removed; explanations belong to the decisions and inspected objects that own
them.

Culture is now persistent local social-historical state. Each local Culture saves
its inherited origin, stable local identity, constituent populations, typed
observations of lived state, recognized practices, transition records, and full
predecessor/evidence/domain provenance. A bounded world-simulation behavior
evaluates elapsed historical cycles. Unchanged history refreshes stable practice
evidence without manufacturing transitions. Meaningful accumulated evidence
deterministically creates a successor Culture, survives serialization, and permits
settlements with a shared inherited origin to diverge through different histories.
Mixed populations remain plural unless lived evidence supports a recorded
synthesis.

The completed acceptance loop is:

`Culture(T) + intervening lived history -> Culture(T+1) -> later choices -> new history`

Spatial planning, ordinary social evaluation, institutional legitimacy and
friction, political development and belief/practice tension, and player-delegated
or NPC settlement development consume substantive current practices. Those
consumers rank otherwise valid possibilities. They do not create authority,
knowledge, land, technology, labor, materials, treasury, or execution capability.
Native jobs and world simulation remain the source of actual effects. Cultural
expression remains as a nonmutating interpretation of Culture in relation to the
society currently expressing it; native `CultureDef` remains one optional visual
inheritance.

The founding flow again coordinates inherited Culture, RimWorld's native
Ideoligion, Political Beliefs, and the actual rules adopted at landing. The native
chooser retains presets, saved Ideoligions, fixed and fluid creation, memes,
precepts, roles, rituals, validation, persistence, and scenario notification.
Entering, returning, and navigating Back preserves the adjacent CA draft and does
not double-notify the scenario. Political Beliefs remain normative; adopted rules,
institutions, and actual practice remain realized state, so agreement and
contradiction both survive.

Existing and founding societies use one ontology at different temporal points.
Established settlements may carry mature institutions and local Culture because
they predate the scenario, but their fixture records an explicit temporal basis
and does not invent unobserved event history. New founders bring inherited state
and establish initial rules; local historical Culture and institutions develop
through play. An explicitly established player-start scenario may retain mature
history, so player identity alone is not the temporal classifier.

The authored composition remains the same test object: three factions, four
settlements, nine population groups, the same world, region, candidate, arrival
tile, and map scale. The selected region contains unsupported world mutators and
therefore remains a transient developer exercise with durable saving disabled.
That condition is preserved rather than hidden or rewritten. It can test creation
and map initialization, while the save/reload runtime case requires a later
compatible-region run.

B7 closes the source and static correction. Operator cases 97-103 and 105 remain
the live creation and behavior observations on this fixture; case 104 remains a
separate compatible-region save/reload observation. Static evidence does not
select options, advance the game, or decide how the result looks and plays.

This batch is closed. `B8` is the next ordinary development batch.
