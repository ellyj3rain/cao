## Record

Runtime behavior now has one typed initiative ladder. Standard preserves ordinary
RimWorld agency, direct and accepted relayed orders, owned continuation, declared
safeguards, and the survival floor. Proactive adds one bounded response to a
current actionable fact. Autonomous adds persistent, adaptive, or coordinated
action within delegated or institutional authority. Legacy Directed and legacy
Standard both migrate to Standard; legacy Proactive and Autonomous retain their
meaning. Pawn defaults, spatial ceilings, menus, saves, and receipts use the same
schema and revision invalidation.

One catalog defines 87 behaviors across 14 realized primary domains and 13
forms. Each definition names its stable key, permission owner or always-available
status, minimum initiative or institutional authority, actor context, evidence,
authority, capability, material constraints, cadence, executor, ownership, and
completion or stand-down condition. Runtime gates, UI descriptions, tracing,
developer censuses, and receipts consume this shared definition rather than
reconstructing behavior from prose or raw integer thresholds.

Permission, initiative, authority, actor-private knowledge, execution
capability, material feasibility, culture, disposition, and direct player
ownership remain independent causes. Knowledge triggers; disposition ranks
permitted choices; authority channels commitments; domain doctrine executes
through native RimWorld jobs, duties, think trees, Lords, social interactions,
and world simulation. Culture can change ranking and expression but cannot
manufacture permission, an office, or resources.

The mixed runtime owners were separated. Hold permission gates hold behavior,
while field medicine, raid response, communication, and draft-chain permissions
own their respective squad-support work. Medic, support, relay, assault,
withdrawal, and other multi-actor commitments record the actual executing
actor's private knowledge, authority, episode, controller, issuer, owner, and
termination. Colony operational access owns shared-item policy; personal threat
equipment remains pawn-initiative-sensitive. Proactive custody may secure and
stabilize; irreversible custody resolution requires Autonomous initiative plus
law, office, or separately receipted unlawful character action.

Player and NPC paths share typed demands, asset capability, siting, culture and
spatial ranking, and native execution, then branch at authorization. Player
spatial work requires explicit authored constraints and delegated authority.
Creation authors confirmed settlement state directly. Later NPC settlement work
requires a current named institutional authority and persists proposer,
approver, labor, beneficiaries, cultural and political basis, owner, episode,
and blockers. Saved jobs and non-job episodes reauthorize their exact current
owner before any durable research, road, repair, rebuild, withdrawal, or home
effect lands.

Settings are grouped by behavior domain and by permission, capability,
presentation, or diagnostic ownership. The selected-pawn Behavior scope and
developer Behavior census are read-only explanations of actual observed
decisions; the settlement census exposes institutional planning without player
autonomy controls. Compact, Standard, and Expanded information detail format the
same state and never alter simulation.

The catalog is built once and uses compact enums and indexed definitions.
Effective pawn profiles cache stable eligibility and invalidate on setting,
initiative, role, and spatial-authority revisions. Existing disposition,
communication, threat, welfare, planning, and detached-snapshot caches remain in
place. Immediate combat stays in native constant-think evaluation; slower
domains keep bounded event or map cadences, and the catalog is not a central
scheduler or per-pawn full-map scoring loop.

Cases 97-105 remain intentionally open for the operator inside RimWorld: reach
map generation; complete the player map; observe optional-asset fallback;
observe Standard native work; observe one bounded Proactive response; observe
one persistent Autonomous objective; confirm direct orders at every tier;
save/reload a registered intent; and inspect the first in-game hour for repeating
exceptions. Static and review evidence establish readiness, not how the game
looks and plays.

This batch is closed. `B7` is the next ordinary development batch.
