## Record

B17 makes no faction owner a first-class value across every persistent inhabited
site already represented by CA. Major settlements and frontier cabins,
homesteads, holdings, and settlements share explicit ownership and material-
support semantics without losing their distinct mechanics. Population groups
retain their own affiliations. Culture, Ideoligion, Political Order,
Technological Knowledge, institutions, organizations, offices, authority,
practice, programs, provisions, and development remain independently owned.
Factionless sites therefore remain complete societies rather than degraded
exceptions.

Detaching an owned site snapshots effective local political, technological, and
institutional state once. Support never becomes ownership, resident affiliation
never infers ownership, and a scale transition never manufactures a faction.
Authoring exposes owner and support directly. Generation, realization, habitat
viability, native adapters, names, summaries, threats, populations, programs,
and persistence consume the same typed relationship. Adjacent-schema migration
validates the full proposed graph before any assignment, so invalid conversion
publishes no partial state.

B17 also generalizes the existing proposition store into an optional broader
pawn-knowledge mode. Observations and reports remain private records with typed
payloads, source-event time, confidence, uncertainty, original source,
immediate reporter, revision, contradiction, supersession, staleness, scope,
and provenance. Communication copies the teller's remembered record; it does
not refresh from world truth. Pawns can therefore know different versions,
retain stale or false affiliation beliefs, and receive later corrective
evidence. Durable and transient fact families keep distinct retention rules.
The readable pawn inspector reports the pawn's belief and provenance rather
than registry implementation.

Campaign catalog 6, pending authoring epoch 15, regional plan schema 16,
settlement population schema 2, frontier holding schema 2, frontier map plan
schema 3, settlement record schema 10, site-link and local-society schema 1, and
proposition-knowledge schema 2 bind the current state. The active fixture is
atomically converted and executable receipts verify affiliation, local social
state, population independence, scale transitions, relay provenance,
contradiction, revision, retention, serialization, and the disabled-mode
boundary while retained suites protect the B10-B16 capability floor.
