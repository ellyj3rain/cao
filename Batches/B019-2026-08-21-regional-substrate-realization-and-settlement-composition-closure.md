The globe shows where towns and cities are; nine world characters are proven separable from one shared table; a generated world states its own realized character in one line.

Regional substrate and tendencies, brought to mechanical truth. Front door - plain-language world character. Settlement composition - culture, roles, style, material, knowledge. One settlement-culture authority is named (the one that already existed), with named culture provenance and one style authority, so a settlement looks like one place and gets the culture its residents brought at generation; culture separates two identical villages and drives a culture-aware material authority (what a people expect a place to be). Material becomes a pure kernel with its own receipts; capacity thresholds work for holdings as well as settlements; a holding's material context stops counting its development level twice; building over time uses what the settlement actually holds and the material authority names the input it does not have; morphology builds from the same resolved material, so what a place builds from is one real question with one answer; urban standing finally raises built complexity. Roles commit works within their people's reach, no role ships without a consequence, and one resolver answers what a materialized settlement knows; what a settlement sows is gated by what its people actually know. A settlement without a culture is still a settlement; CAO failing is not the player's world failing; the assembly reports its own identity and the build log names which assembly ran.
