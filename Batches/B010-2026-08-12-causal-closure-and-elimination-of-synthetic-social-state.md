## Record

B10 closes the aggregate causal ownership boundary before operator runtime
validation. A semantic fact now exists because a named owner persists the facts
and lifecycle that constitute it. Hashes and randomness remain available for
equivalent names, forms, layouts, material candidates, and bounded scheduling
after eligibility; they do not form memberships, institutions, authority,
ownership, knowledge, Culture, Political Beliefs, capability, or history.

Domestic membership is persistent and sourced. Native partner, kin, shared-bed,
shared-residence, or explicit authored co-residence evidence may form a domestic
unit; represented transitions change or dissolve it. Population adjacency, pawn
ordering, fixed group size, and a stable hash cannot. Residents without a valid
multi-pawn unit retain individual domestic self-provision. Residence assignments
are typed, persist their source, and reconcile native faction and settlement
events before domestic and institutional consumers run.

Practiced capability is a read model over domain evidence. Medicine,
production, logistics, civic administration, research, security, commerce, and
communications record the actual actors, organizations, work, knowledge,
material nodes, history, blockers, confidence, assessment tick, and source
signature relevant to that domain. Capability cannot authorize behavior or
create a program. Broad world tendencies likewise remain read summaries or
generation pressures at their owning surface; all cycles in which a summary
created the reality it later summarized are removed.

Every active settlement program now begins with an explicit need and exact
operator where institutional, plus standing, knowledge, labor, material,
activity, target, access, funding or maintenance, failure state, and runtime
consumer. The Starting Region editor owns establishment and removal of initial
program facts. Its pure formation kernel is also the only translation used by
the governed fixture. Entries preserve key-plus-operator cardinality, and
runtime work retains the exact program key, operator identity, and signature
through research, cultivation, roads, repair, rebuilding, and completion.

Materialization records one typed receipt for every Thing or zone role under the
exact program, operator, and signature. Destruction invalidates the real node;
successful rebuilding atomically rebinds the receipt, saved program identity,
and any provision holding. An asset never proves the institution using it.
Provision materialization preflights every node, records every required role and
stock identity, and rolls the entire mutation back on failure. Observed stock is
live, unique, located on settlement ground, and reachable by the actual
consumers. Program kind and operator must match the provision arrangement
exactly; no generic materializer fallthrough exists.

Security practices are produced from a live Defense program, its exact operator
and assets, typed resident labor, and armed assigned guards. Patrols consume
only live assigned guards. Research requires a saved Research program, its exact
bench receipt, a qualified typed resident, and an active question or project
before work begins. Hostile encounters consume only already practiced doctrine;
they do not synthesize a doctrine or use a hash to decide whether it applies.

Political Beliefs remain normative positions about legitimate order. Current
offices, policies, authority, ownership, taxation, work, and provision remain
separately instituted facts. Culture uses one namespaced subject for one
population to affect meaning, demand, legitimacy, participation, prestige,
stigma, form, maintenance, adoption, resistance, and historical transition; it
does not grant authority, knowledge, labor, technology, land, material, office,
or execution capability.

The governed recovery composition is translated into 19 explicit current-schema
facts: four Food preparation, four Storage, four Medicine, two Production, four
Gathering, and one Custody operation, each with its actual population-group
operator. No obsolete facility mask is restored. Unsupported program and
provision forms remain absent and state the missing contract. Both active plan
surfaces agree byte for byte and enter the same production realization path as
operator-authored state.

The modular guardrail is satisfied through subordinate owners for domestic
state and formation, residence adaptation, capability evidence and assessment,
program operational facts and runtime validation, program assets, provision
operator/funding/material/access/runtime services, and security assignment.
These services run through existing event and regional reconciliation cadences;
no new independent tick loop was introduced. The detailed impact ledger records
which existing modules grew or narrowed and leaves measured post-B10
decomposition candidates to B11.

The repeated sweep leaves no unresolved Critical or High synthetic-state
finding. Static evidence establishes causal, persistence, build, and fixture
readiness. It does not establish how the creation flow or generated game looks
and plays. This batch is closed; `B11` is next, but the next action is the
operator's aggregate runtime validation.
