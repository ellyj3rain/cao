## Record

B9 closes Starting Region as an object-led authoring surface. Objects and Map
remain canonical navigation. Selecting a faction or settlement from Objects opens
its Details; map selection remains spatial and exposes `View Details`. Compact
settlement Details keeps the selected object visible and provides previous and
next comparison in object-rail order without regenerating state. Conditional
choice presentation renders a control only when more than one valid choice
exists, a concise fact when one essential choice exists, and a warning only when
no valid choice blocks confirmation.

Settlement Details now presents identity, Population, Culture, and Settlement
Composition in that order. Faction Details presents identity and technology,
Population and Culture, Beliefs and Current Order, Settlement Authority, Faction
Relations, and Settlements. Region Details preserves geographic and regional
inspection. Backend source labels, generation provenance, schema language,
single-option menus, architectural explanations, settlement-level faction
relations, and redundant provision, material, research, and route sections no
longer occupy ordinary player UI. Measured text and row heights replace the
fixed-height assumptions covered by the deterministic layout receipts.

The seven-bit Starting Facilities ontology and its persisted masks are retired
without aliases or migration. A namespaced registry now exposes 22 functional
programs over housing, food, storage, medicine, production, trade, governance,
security, defense, research, religion, social life, culture, agriculture,
communications, and transport. Each program records its sources,
applicability, native candidate-resolution contract, materialization contract,
inspection summary, runtime or maintenance consumer, fallback, and stable
receipt. Programs can require rooms, buildings, worktables, storage, operators,
stock, access, and linked assets rather than standing for one decorative object.

Candidate discovery reads loaded functional contracts. Native worktables,
storage, research, medical, gathering, recreation, communication, animal,
room-role, placement, and related building contracts participate independent of
content pack. Ported `Anon2CushionedChair` and `DankPyon_Bust` participate through
the same loaded functional evidence; visual-only props do not establish a
program. Materialization records actual placed identities and consumes confirmed
program state instead of rerolling authoring.

Provisioning now admits only household, communal, and authority arrangements.
Every realized arrangement owns an actual operator, access, material support,
funding, and behavioral consumer, and registered arrangement counts survive
round-trip exactly. Unsupported vendor, religious, dues, and abstract distribution
branches were removed rather than advertised. Faction technology remains faction
state; settlement research appears only when a real research program and loaded
research asset support it.

Culture onboarding presents meanings as semantic cards and guided questions.
Exact continuous values remain available through contextual fine tuning and
round-trip without becoming the default language of Culture. Practices and the
causal preview remain distinct from meanings, native Ideoligion, Political
Beliefs, and instituted order.

The governed fixture contains 15 programs and three provision arrangements at
Megaeth, 17 and three at Red Cervexa, 13 and two at Tascan Bramble, and 13 and one
at Black Delta. Both active plan surfaces agree byte for byte and retain their
world, region, candidate, arrival, scale, faction, settlement, and population
identity under the current schema.

Static evidence leaves five B9 observations to the operator: compact and wide
Details layout at runtime, selection and comparison feel, Faction Relations as
the visual reference, and reopening exact-value Culture fine tuning without
loss. B9 is the final pre-runtime correction batch. It establishes readiness;
only the operator establishes how the creation flow and generated game look and
play.

This batch is closed. `B10` is the next ordinary development batch and must not
begin before the B9 operator runtime test.
