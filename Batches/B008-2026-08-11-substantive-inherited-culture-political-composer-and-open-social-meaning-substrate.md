## Record

B8 establishes substantive Culture at the authoring boundary. A Culture now
contains stable inherited and local identity, constituent shares, inherited and
current social meanings, inherited and lived practices, observations,
transitions, predecessor and evidence signatures, maturity, temporal basis, and
an optional native visual tradition kept explicitly separate. Name plus visual
tradition cannot satisfy validation. Saved Culture profiles copy reusable
inherited meanings, practices, and optional visual tradition without locality,
observations, transitions, or a live profile identity.

The social subject contract is open and namespaced. Twelve initial registered
subjects each identify a factual source and at least one real consumer; unknown
valid keys survive persistence, while inert unregistered strings do not enter an
editor. Meanings store approval, normality, prestige, salience, population scope,
provenance, and evidence identity for one concrete subject. Practices remain
durable conduct rather than being fabricated from selected meanings.

The Culture composer presents overview, social meanings, inherited practices,
visual tradition, and a causal preview. Founders and established factions use
the same substantive model at their different temporal boundaries. Established
settlements may receive direct authored local baselines, including plurality and
disagreement, without fabricated transitions. A new player settlement carries
inherited Culture and develops local history through play.

Political authoring is question-first across all thirteen normative questions.
The five built-in profiles are complete, parentless, distinct vectors; applying
one copies authored answers and creates no runtime profile identity. Missing
player answers are never filled randomly. NPC derivation records a stable causal
receipt for each resolved axis and leaves unsupported axes unset. Political
Beliefs remain normative state distinct from current structure, institutions,
founding rules, Culture, and native Ideoligion.

Registered social facts can produce pawn-local interpretation only when the pawn
has knowledge. Culture, Political Beliefs, Ideoligion where it overlaps, personal
state, institutions, and relationships contribute without erasing disagreement.
Actual informed pawn reactions aggregate into subject-specific participation,
mean response, dispersion, polarization, influential minorities, alignment, and
cross-group dissonance. Repeated qualified evidence across historical periods may
change later Culture; one isolated event, unchanged evidence, or opening an
editor cannot.

Culture consumers now query the generic meaning resolver. Spatial,
institutional, political, social, and settlement-development code may rank or
interpret otherwise available action, but Culture grants no knowledge,
permission, authority, office, technology, labor, land, material, treasury, or
execution capability. Abstract stock icons no longer stand for Culture,
Political Beliefs, profiles, founding rules, or cultural expression; native
Ideoligion retains its actual symbol, and concrete object and action icons remain.

The pre-release persistence boundary is intentionally destructive. Authoring
epoch 8 clears incompatible CA-owned Culture, Political Beliefs, profile,
founding-draft, social-interpretation, and regional-authoring state with one clear
diagnostic. No field-by-field migration, compatibility alias, partial preset
inheritance, or abandoned profile identity remains. The B8 fixture is generated
directly from its intentional world and region inputs.

The selected region remains a transient developer exercise because it contains
unsupported world mutators. It can exercise creation, map initialization, and
behavior cases 97–103 and 105. Behavior case 104 remains a separate
compatible-region save/reload observation. These runtime judgments belong to the
operator; the build, receipts, reviews, and deployment establish readiness only.

This batch is closed. `B9` is the next ordinary development batch.
