# B13 Culture causal contract

Status: canonical for B13

Culture is a population distribution over twenty-four explicit questions. It
does not own doctrine, political rules, institutions, concrete practices, or
historical events. Those facts remain with Ideoligion, Political Beliefs,
adopted order and organizations, practice history, and their respective event
owners. Culture receives represented evidence from those owners and changes
how pawns appraise and respond to it.

## Registry shape

The production registry contains three questions in each of eight categories:

| Category | Questions |
|---|---|
| Relationships, family, sexuality | same-sex acceptance; plurality acceptance; kin obligation |
| Gender and social authority | gender distribution in authority; gendered work; office access |
| Status and hierarchy | hereditary legitimacy; rank differentiation; status mobility |
| Membership and outsiders | outsider inclusion; integration preference; membership access |
| Public authority and social order | inclusion in public voice; dissent tolerance; enforcement legitimacy |
| Property, labor, provision | coercive labor legitimacy; mutual provision obligation; property control |
| Violence, captivity, punishment | captive protection; punishment severity; retaliatory violence |
| Knowledge and tradition | knowledge access; novelty acceptance; expertise deference |

Every question owns five ordered semantic anchors, an editable population
center, a distribution spread, causal provenance, represented evidence routes,
and substantive consumers. Exact keys, anchors, sources, and consumers are
recorded in `CULTURE_RESEARCH_CORPUS.md` and enforced by the production registry.

## Authoring contract

Manual editing, historical and social presets, deterministic randomization, and
completion of unset generated state all write the same `CACulture` question
distributions. There is no preset mode, custom mode, or blend object. Applying
a preset supplies all twenty-four rows; later editing changes the selected row.
The explicit Randomize action replaces all twenty-four question rows on the
same Culture object. Generated completion is the separate path that fills only
missing rows, preserves authored values and provenance, and does not perturb
unrelated fixed-seed results.

One visible global diversity control supplies the default within-population
spread. An expanded question may own an advanced spread override. Changing
either spread never moves a question center. Concrete practices remain
read-only historical facts and are not Culture controls.

## Causal separations

| Cause | Direct effect | State it does not own |
|---|---|---|
| Question position | population center on the ordered scale | a pawn's exact sampled position |
| Global diversity | default distribution spread | any question center |
| Per-question spread | advanced local spread override | other rows or global diversity |
| Salience | attention to the question | moral conviction |
| Position extremity, identity, doctrine, lived evidence | moral conviction | attention frequency |
| Norm strength and divergence tolerance | perceived social pressure | expected enforcement |
| Represented rules, sanctions, and enforcement history | expected enforcement | population norm strength |
| Source confidence | strength of the inherited prior | current knowledge confidence |
| Direct evidence, source appraisal, and epistemic vigilance | knowledge confidence | inherited-prior strength |
| Visibility | likelihood that an expression is observed | compression of public expression |
| Question-specific psychology | small bounded deviation in private position | a replacement Culture identity or personality score |
| Demonstrated domain expertise | source weighting under expertise deference | truth or access by itself |

These values persist separately in the cultural-cognition owner. Generation and
runtime consumers read the realized fields; they do not reroll or infer them
from neighboring controls.

Pawn Culture resolution carries population identity, local reaction scope, and
institutional jurisdiction as separate facts. Regional pawns use the exact
settlement organization already resolved by their population assignment for
sanction and legitimacy appraisal; cognition never reconstructs jurisdiction
from an unrelated membership scan. Player social history remains scoped to the
current settlement while the player organization remains the institution.

## Evidence and feedback

Seven questions receive direct represented-fact observations: known unions,
plural unions, officeholder gender, office access, work assignment by gender,
mixed-population integration, and recorded dissent sanctions. The other
seventeen use exact factual-subject adapters. An absent event is not negative
evidence. Each direct observation persists its directional position,
dispersion, represented pawn count, eligible population, continuity, and source
signature in the Culture observation ledger. A stable per-pawn sample then
feeds source appraisal and lived moral evidence from that same record; load does
not reroll uptake or create a second evidence owner. Observations persist before
longitudinal transition and require the registered continuity and evidence
thresholds.

The social-reaction owner keeps a runtime-only per-pawn index over its persisted
ledger. Event ingress updates it, load and retention rebuild it from authority,
and daily question refreshes reuse the indexed ordered reactions rather than
rescanning the global retained ledger for each question.

Every built-in factual subject maps to one or more demonstrated RimWorld skill
domains. Expertise deference reads the source pawn's represented skill in those
domains; unregistered integration subjects retain a neutral prior rather than
borrowing an unrelated skill.

Every question reaches at least one material consumer in relationship,
household, office, work, membership, public-order, property, provision,
violence, custody, knowledge, political, or institutional appraisal. Culture
changes appraisal and bounded CA-originated discretionary selection. Native
legality, capability, material conditions, saved operator intent, and direct
orders remain authoritative.

## Persistence and generation

Culture remains schema 10 with question registry 2. Cultural cognition is
schema 2 in campaign catalog 3 and persists attention, inherited-prior
strength, perceived social pressure, observation likelihood, private position,
public expression, confidence, conviction, and uncertainty as distinct facts.
Starting Region completion and broader non-player world-faction generation use
the same registry and completion kernel. Player Culture is never silently
randomized after authoring.

The governed fixture contains eight complete Culture records, each with all
twenty-four root distributions, while retaining the authored local rows and
legacy evidence that still have valid current-schema meaning. Static acceptance
is recorded in `B13_ACCEPTANCE_RECEIPTS.md`; calibration limits and source
provenance are recorded in `CULTURE_RESEARCH_CORPUS.md`.
